# Good Ground curriculum review — 2026-10-28

Curriculum 8. Reviewed all 154 existing skills and all 384 previous active steps, including setup, teaching, advancement, easier options, prerequisite logic, appropriateness and claim scope. The result is 154 skills, 386 current steps, 45 historical steps and 87 source records. Two context-transfer steps were added to Leave it. Nine appliance steps were retired and nine training-prop steps received new IDs.

## Standard and limits

Use the best applicable evidence and current humane veterinary guidance, weighing methods, access and external validity. Not every published recommendation agrees; unsupported or contradictory recommendations are not adopted. No study validates every custom app sequence, numerical criterion, or dependency threshold. This review is not a veterinary specialist endorsement or a trial in dogs. Sources checked only as abstracts or selected sections are identified individually. The AKC breed-name directory is profile terminology, not training evidence.

The independently checked research library in the central Archives was used with its corrections and exclusions. Only relevant annotations were added to the bundled register. P51 legacy corrections/leadership guidance was excluded. The older P26 sound booklet remains a historical reference but its no-comfort advice is not adopted or cited as a current procedure. No shock-collar efficacy result was silently erased; its narrow design and welfare/generalization limits remain explicit.

## Material decisions

- Keep useful cues and teach offered choices in specific contexts; neither supports an unattended hazard guarantee. Leave it gains separate offered-choice and small handler-distance practice.
- Correct indoor-versus-outdoor crossing gates, independent departure-action and sound conditioning, and the optional visitor-view gate. Storm-plan readiness does not require a real event.
- Reinforce comfortably noticed triggers without requiring obedience. Distinguish trained postures from emotional comfort, and honor whole-body withdrawal during care.
- Deliver terminal marker rewards promptly rather than requiring another response. Make a photo before marking the completed brief pose.
- Balance target-hand availability in both picture-card conditions. Describe familiar-odor-versus-blank learning accurately, with no generalized scent-matching or operational tracking claim.
- Replace refrigerator opening, access and closure with supervised lightweight purpose-built training-prop tasks. This is a conservative curriculum-design choice for a general pet app, not a claim that research proves all supervised appliance work harmful. Preserve every original step and record without granting new exercise credit.
- Keep concrete practice criteria, with visible explanation that numbers and session checks are adjustable editorial targets. Comfort and recovery override quotas.

## Other training-advice surfaces

Also reviewed all 14 tailored practice alternatives, three optional support preferences, four deliberately limited breed/survey prompts, the 13 tutorial pages, trigger-plan instructions and generated journal guidance. Breed alone changes neither instruction nor progress. File/function fingerprints cover the shared supplementary advice.
