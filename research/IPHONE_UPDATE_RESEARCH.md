# iPhone update research — 21 October 2026

The update uses original synthesized marker sounds. No third-party audio is copied.

## Maintenance reminders

- [Demant et al. (2011), publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S016815911100181X): indexed abstract and conclusion snippets reviewed; full paper not reviewed in this update. A laboratory obedience task remained learned at the four-week test. This provides no universal forgetting deadline. Source R13.
- [Smith et al., detection learning](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0168159124003228): indexed abstract/highlights reviewed; full paper not reviewed. Scheduling effects depended on the detection task, breaks and individual characteristics. This does not supply an interval for pet-dog refreshers. Source R27.
- [RSPCA training guidance](https://www.rspca.org.uk/adviceandwelfare/pets/dogs/training): public page reviewed. Supports brief, regular reward-based practice, with no fixed retention deadline. Source P33.

The seven-day default is explicitly a product choice. Users can choose 14 days, 30 days, or off. Reminders cover learned foundation/walk skills and selected transfer programs, excluding automatic fear exposure. A consistency timestamp starts the initial clock without being labeled a practice log. Only linked skill-practice entries establish a logged practice date; note edits, clicker presses and future-dated sessions do not count. Elapsed days use calendar dates to avoid daylight-saving errors. Missing logs are never presented as proof of forgetting.

## Individual tailoring

The prior breed-specific copy was removed. Existing genomic and survey sources remain as research records, including their original publication titles. No dog is assigned anxiety, motivation, health needs or temperament from breed alone.

- [Morrill et al. (2022), public manuscript](https://www.zoology.ubc.ca/~otto/veg/Readings/morrill2022ancestry.pdf), summary and individual prediction results rechecked: substantial individual variation limits prediction from breed. R08.
- [Salonen et al. (2020), publisher PDF](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-59837-z.pdf), results/discussion and limitations inspected: invitations cover reported sound sensitivity in Lagotto Romagnolo and Soft Coated Wheaten Terrier, and fearfulness in Spanish Water Dog and Shetland Sheepdog. Self-selection, owner reports and group differences limit interpretation. R09.
- [Dogs Trust support guidance](https://www.dogstrust.org.uk/dog-advice/understanding-your-dog/signs-your-dog-may-be-stressed), relevant sections reviewed: support space, choice, gradual introductions and veterinary assessment. P34. Existing P26 and R17 support the original sound/handling approach.
- [AKC directory](https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/) consulted for terminology. The 209-name convenience catalog uses factual common names and permits free-text mixes. No temperament descriptions, ranking or registry images are copied. P35.

Only owner-confirmed observations affect suggestions and setup notes. Owners of every breed can choose the same support preferences. Declining or closing a breed question leaves traits unchanged; the question is remembered to avoid repeated prompts. Original lesson criteria stay visible. No unvalidated “three sets instead of two” rule, nutrition prescription, trait labeled “lazy/stubborn”, or automatic fear exposure was introduced.
